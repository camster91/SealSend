# SealSend iOS and Android research

Research date: 11 October 2026. Status: source-backed discovery and implemented first candidate; no customer validation. The web product remains production; this native candidate is local on `feat/native-mobile-apps`.

## Who this version serves

Primary hypothesis: a casual host organizing a birthday, dinner, club meetup, or small celebration wants to create a clear invitation, collect replies across phone platforms, and recognize arrivals without managing a spreadsheet. The host is the app user; guests should not need another account or an app installation to reply. Secondary segment: a volunteer running arrivals for a small gathering. Professional planners require team discovery, multiple events, messaging, exports and branding; those workflows already have broader web coverage and are not claimed as native parity in this first increment.

This segmentation is an inference from the product's current free-beta limits and competitor offerings, not a measured market result. No analytics or interviews were obtained for this research. Do not call it product-market fit or claim a quantified acquisition benefit.

## Method and confidence

Compared official product/help pages and current Apple/Google listings for five direct competitors. Reviewed a small purposive sample of visible store feedback for friction hypotheses, not a statistically representative review corpus. Store ratings, review visibility, regional prices and app behavior can change. A complaint describes that person's experience, not verified reproducibility in the current competitor release. Older reviews below are explicitly historical. Developer replies matter, especially where they clarify a pricing allegation. No competitor purchase, personal account creation or destructive testing was performed.

Web preview cannot prove this. |

## Product and architecture decisions

Use a shared Expo/React Native native application, not a WebView of the marketing site. SDK57 currently pairs React19.2.3 with RN0.86 and requires modern Node/Xcode; platform minimums are Android7/iOS16.4 per [Expo's compatibility table](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/). Keep native dependency locking independent of Next's newer React tree.

Native credential transport requires a versioned, strictly allowlisted host gateway. Reuse existing event ownership, quotas, publication readiness and check-in business handlers. Keep the browser's cookie authentication and origin protection unchanged. A server-created AsyncLocalStorage context supplies only the identity validated from the revocable database session. No incoming identity header is trusted. Gateway defaults off until a reviewed backend beta release.

Store only credentials with [SecureStore](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/securestore/). No contact upload, notification permission or guest-list cache in v0.1. Camera is requested at scanning time with name search as fallback. [Camera](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/camera/) and the [calendar composer](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/calendar/) add useful native functionality; calendar data is not queried and broad read/write calendar permissions are blocked. OS sharing hands control to the human; it does not automatically send invitations.

Visual direction: digital shapes, clear type, warm direct copy and restrained haptics after confirmed arrivals. No paper/envelope metaphor. Avoid confetti or motion that obscures check-in; respect native font scaling. No fabricated guest lists are shown in the product.

## Pricing, growth and validation

Recommendation: keep this first private native beta within the already enforced free host plan, with no new charges. Cost assumptions still need actual monthly hosting/provider/support figures. A free native download does not remove backend cost; avoid declaring a viable unit-economics model until event/guest/provider usage is measured. For paid native access, resolve store billing rules and cancellation/deletion compatibility first.

Initial distribution hypothesis: the existing marketing pages lead interested hosts to an explicitly limited beta; shared invitations acquire guests through the web. Measure first successful published event, shared link, first RSVP, check-in completion and repeat event, with consent/privacy safeguards. No store listing or public beta invitation is authorized by this implementation request.

Human study plan: five casual hosts and two volunteer arrivals staff, mixed iOS/Android and small/large text settings. Ask each to create/edit/publish/share an event with a fabricated guest, recover after a dropped connection, find/check in/undo an arrival, decline camera access, archive and create another event, then find deletion/support. Observe completion, assistance, confusion and time; do not invent targets achieved. Suggested beta threshold: every critical task completes without assistance; no lost/duplicate mutation; no ownership leak; no inaccessible control. Recruit/contact people only after explicit authorization. Expert/agent reviews can find bugs but cannot substitute for these users.

## Store constraints to resolve before submission

[Apple review rules](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/uk/) require substantive functionality, privacy, appropriate billing and moderation where user-generated content applies. [Apple deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion/) requires deletion initiation within apps that create accounts. [Google deletion guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en) also requires an accessible web deletion route and data-safety disclosure. Re-check [Google's current developer policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18258653?hl=en-ph&rd=1) and exact target-API deadline at submission; Expo targeting API36 is not store acceptance evidence.

Remaining work: signed native binary/device QA, reviewed backend activation, account ownership/bundle registrations, privacy labels, deletion-job proof, review access, store screenshots/metadata, and small real-user pilot. Push notifications, photos/social tools, native account export, paid billing, shared-team discovery and rich invitation design are intentionally later increments, with separate acceptance criteria. The first increment must be reliable before broadening it.
