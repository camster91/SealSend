# Competitive positioning
Primary websites reviewed October10,2026. Qualitative positioning only: no authority scores, keyword volumes, traffic estimates or market-share claims. Features/prices can change; verify again before publishing a comparison.

| Product | Observed positioning and content | Implication for SealSend |
|---|---|---|
| Partiful | Online RSVP landing page; free digital invitations and guests replying without account/app | Free and no-account RSVP are category expectations, not unique claims |
| Luma | Pricing page lists free unlimited events/guests, check-in and paid-event options | Do not compete on unlimited volume or ticketing during the100-guest beta |
| Paperless Post | Occasion/design collections, online invitations, email/text/link sharing and RSVP tracking | Do not imply SealSend is a stationery service; focus on digital workflow |
| Evite | Free invitation library and occasion-based content plus guest replies | Broad birthday searches have established competitors; start with narrower intent |
| Joy | Wedding websites, online invitations and multi-event RSVP tools | Do not start with a claim to replace complete wedding planning suites |

Sources:

Opportunity hypothesis: personal help and a clear invitation-to-arrival workflow for small clubs and community gatherings. Test this with actual organizers. None of this establishes that SealSend has a unique moat, cheaper total cost or a better product than these competitors.
